# Educationpathway 
EducationPathway Sri Lanka is a web-based platform designed to address this gap. It provides personalized guidance, course recommendations, and multi-pathway analysis for students based on their A-Level subject streams,Z-scores, and career interests. By integrating government university requirements, private institutions, and vocational training options, EducationPathway empowers students to make informed, confident, and future-oriented decisions about their academic andprofessional journeys.
